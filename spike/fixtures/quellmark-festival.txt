The Quellmark Lantern Festival (synthetic spike fixture, fictional event)

The Quellmark Lantern Festival is held every year in the fictional town of Ostrevin on the second Thursday of November. It was founded in 1964 by a ferry captain named Idris Vellacourt. The festival's signature event is the release of exactly 317 paper lanterns over the Ostrevin harbour at dusk. The official festival colour is teal, and the festival mascot is a heron named Pell.
